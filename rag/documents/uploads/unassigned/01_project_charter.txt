PROJECT CHARTER (TEMPLATE)
Project: AI-Driven Enterprise Project Intelligence & Risk Management Platform

1. PURPOSE
Give project managers and executives a single platform that predicts project risks early, explains why they are happening, and recommends actions, using both structured project data and unstructured project documents.

2. OBJECTIVES
- Predict schedule delay and cost overrun probability for every active project.
- Maintain a live, scored risk register across the project portfolio.
- Provide a natural-language assistant (RAG) that answers questions from project documents with source citations.
- Reduce the time spent on manual status reporting by at least 40 percent.

3. SCOPE
In scope: data ingestion from project tools, risk scoring models, RAG assistant, dashboards, alerts.
Out of scope: replacing the existing project scheduling tool, financial accounting.

4. STAKEHOLDERS
Sponsor: Chief Operations Officer. Users: Project Managers, PMO, Risk Officers, Executives.

5. KEY DELIVERABLES
Data pipeline, risk prediction models, RAG knowledge base, web dashboard, user documentation.

6. SUCCESS CRITERIA
Risk prediction recall above 80 percent on historical projects; RAG answers grounded in cited documents; adoption by at least 70 percent of active project managers.
