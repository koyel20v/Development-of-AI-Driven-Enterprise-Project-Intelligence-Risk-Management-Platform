LESSONS LEARNED (SAMPLE)
Project: Customer Portal (closed)

WHAT WENT WELL
Daily standups kept teams aligned. Early user prototypes reduced rework.

WHAT WENT WRONG
Security testing was left to the final month and revealed critical defects, delaying launch by 5 weeks. Requirements kept changing without impact analysis, causing budget overrun of 12 percent.

ROOT CAUSES
No security testing in the sprint cycle. No formal change control process.

RECOMMENDATIONS
1. Run security scans every sprint.
2. Require impact analysis before accepting any scope change.
3. Reserve 10 to 15 percent schedule buffer for integration and security fixes.
4. Escalate any task more than 5 days late in the first week it slips.
